# Plan v2, Q3 results: Sloan's two suggestions applied to the v1 fire model (2 Nov 2026)

Plan: `docs/research_plan_v2.md` (frozen 2 Nov 2026, f9c3685). Code: `src/v2_q3.py`. Numbers: `results/v2_q3_results.csv` and `results/v2_q3_yearly.csv`.

Sample: 24 seasons, 2002 to 2025. Fire energy is the Aug–Oct sum of MODIS fire radiative power (FRP, MW) in the v1 island boxes. Rain is GPCP v2.3, the three v1 cells per island. All models include the post-2016 step, as in v1.

## Primary tests

p-values are unadjusted. The plan applies the Holm correction over T1–T6 once T1 and T2 exist. All four p-values below are smaller than 0.05/6.

| Test | Rain measure | Rain coefficient (SE) | p | Bootstrap 95% | Fire energy for less rain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T3 Sumatra | Aug–Oct mean, mm/day | −0.202 (0.030) | 1.2e-6 | −0.266 to −0.131 | ×1.59 per 1 mm/day |
| T5 Sumatra | Jun–Oct total, mm | −0.00178 (0.00027) | 1.4e-6 | −0.0025 to −0.0012 | ×1.51 per 100 mm |
| T4 Kalimantan | Aug–Oct mean, mm/day | −0.167 (0.015) | 1.7e-10 | −0.195 to −0.142 | ×1.47 per 1 mm/day |
| T6 Kalimantan | Jun–Oct total, mm | −0.00135 (0.00011) | 4.0e-11 | −0.0017 to −0.0012 | ×1.36 per 100 mm |

Leaving out one year at a time, the coefficients stay between −0.215 and −0.169 (T3), −0.00197 and −0.00148 (T5), −0.170 and −0.155 (T4), and −0.00151 and −0.00126 (T6). The largest p-value in any of these runs is 1e-5.

The two rain measures are on different scales (a daily rate against a seasonal total), so the factors in the last column cannot be compared with each other. The plan does not compare their fit, and this note does not either.

## Comparison with v1 (detection counts) on the same 24 seasons

| Island | Counts, per 1 mm/day less (Aug–Oct) | FRP, same model |
|---|---|---|
| Sumatra | ×1.49 | ×1.59 |
| Kalimantan | ×1.43 | ×1.47 |

Measured by fire energy instead of detections, the rain effect is the same direction and a little larger on both islands.

The post-2016 step is larger for fire energy than for counts in Sumatra: after 2016, fire energy is 0.13 times the earlier level at the same Aug–Oct rain (counts, v1 on 2001–2025: 0.23). In Kalimantan it is 0.41 (counts: 0.53). As the v1 post-hoc checks showed, the size of this step depends on the break year chosen.

Singapore link (v1 H4 with FRP instead of counts): Spearman ρ = 0.93 between the two islands' Aug–Oct FRP sum and Singapore's Aug–Oct mean PM2.5, 2016–2025 (10 seasons, p 1e-4). v1 with counts gave 0.88.

## What this does and does not show

Following Sloan's two suggestions does not change the v1 conclusion: drier seasons have more fire, whether fire is counted or measured by energy, and whether rain is taken over Aug–Oct or summed from June. The evidence remains correlational, from 24 seasons, on data we had partly seen before the plan was frozen (see the plan's honesty statement). The analysis does not model non-linear rain beyond the log form.
